# Demo pricing policy

This synthetic policy is an ingestion and citation example. It does not modify executable
Commerce rules, which remain configured and enforced independently.

## Advertised price and margin

Respect each product's minimum advertised price. Maintain a gross margin rate of at least
15%, calculated as (selling price minus unit cost) divided by selling price.

## Price stability

Limit a proposed price change to 20% of the current price. A smaller package in the same
product family must not cost more than a larger package. Review unusual market conditions
before changing prices.

## Human oversight

Quantitative previews do not authorize a price change. Grounded explanations, independent
validation, and a recorded approval decision are required by the target workflow before
future platform execution can be enabled.
